# BuildZone

A first-person, multiplayer creative sandbox that runs in the browser. Explore a town,
customize your character, drive cars, build anything and play with friends.

- **Towns to explore**: a main town around the spawn square plus four villages, joined by roads,
  with houses you can walk into, shops, parks, playgrounds, a beach and street lamps
- **A big world beyond the towns** (1280 × 1280 blocks, and you can build up to 128 high):
  - **The outer lands** (since v0.44, all round the outside of the old world): the **Outer Loop**, a
    ring road all the way round, with roads out to it (east past the quarry, west past the farm,
    north through the snowy woods), trees and wildflower meadows, and the bay at Sunny Shores
    carrying on south to a far beach. New places go along the Outer Loop:
    - **FreshMart Grocery** 🛒 (since v0.45, on the Outer Loop, by the road out east): fruit
      stands and shelves of food. Talk to Gus to buy something to eat (hungry? you eat it right away; full up, it goes in

    - **BuildZone Airport** ✈️ (since v0.48, beside the Outer Loop): a long runway with a dashed
      center line, stripes at the ends and edge lights, an apron where four **airplanes**

    - **The BuildZone Zoo** 🦁🐘 (since v0.53, on the north side of the Outer Loop): seven pens

    - **The Fire Station** 🚒 (since v0.54, on the south side of the Outer Loop): red brick, with

      on a phone, the ⭐ button), **G** turns on the lights and siren 🚨, and **G**
      again the **hose** too 💦: it sprays water ahead of the truck (steer to aim). **Fires** only
      happen while somebody's driving a fire truck (since v0.55.4): a little while after you get
      in, a call comes in, and a fire breaks out on a road in town (just flames and smoke: nothing

      Chief Flint is out front
    - **The Loop Express** 🚆 (since v0.51; two tracks and real stations since v0.55): an
      elevated railway all the way round the Outer Loop, high over the outside of the road on
      concrete pillars. There are **two tracks**: on the inner one the trains go round clockwise,

      slows in, stops, opens its doors (ding-dong!), waits about ten seconds, beeps, shuts them
      and pulls away. The stations are island platforms between the two tracks, with a roof,
      lamps, benches and the station's name, and **stairs** down to the ground. No tickets: climb
      the stairs, wait on the platform (the hint at the bottom says how many seconds till the next
      train each way), **walk in** through the open doors, ride (walk about the car, or right-click

      station you want. The hint on the train
      says where it stops next. Everyone sees the same trains. (The old blue ticket machines still

    - **Friendship Park** 🤝 (since v0.50, on the north side of the Outer Loop): paths and flower

  - **Up north, the snowy country** (the ground turns to snow and snowflakes start to fall): a

  - **Out west, the woods**: four **log cabins** along a forest road (beds, tables, chimneys,

  - **Down south, Sunny Shores**: a sandy beach on the sea (beach umbrellas, palm trees, a

  - **Out east, Big Rock Quarry**: a stepped stone pit with **dump trucks**. Drive one in under the

  - **Out west, past the woods, Happy Acres Farm**: a red barn (with a pickup truck inside and hay

  - **Up north**, more snowy pine forest past the ski mountain
  - **Lots of open land** all around, with trees and flowers, for building
  - Roads lead out to all of it
- **Skis** (take a pair from the rack in the ski lodge; they're yours to keep, and you wear them):
  on snow they glide and pick up speed downhill. Hold W to carve toward where you're looking, A/D
  to lean, S to snowplough (slow down), Space to jump. Powder sprays up behind you. **Ice** is
  slippery even without skis
- **Fishing**: the docks (in town and at the big lake) have fishing stools. Sit on one
  (right-click), face the water and click to cast. Wait for the bobber to dip, then click again,
  quick! Minnows, sunfish, perch, puffer fish, rainbow trout, crabs, the odd old boot, and (if
  you're lucky) a Golden Koi or a Giant Catfish. Each kind is worth coins, and the game counts
  how many kinds you've caught. Fishing Stools are one of the Chair's styles, so you can build
  your own fishing spot by any water
- **A job at the Pizza Palace**: talk to Chef Rosa (or right-click the pizza oven) and she gives
  you a hot pizza to deliver. A glowing beam with a 🍕 shows which house it goes to, and a banner
  shows how far it is and how long you have for a tip. Walk, run or drive there (you carry the
  pizza box, so everyone can see you're on the job) and get 4 to 8 coins, plus 2 more if you're quick
- **Signs you can write on** (🛋️ Home tab): put one on a wall and type what it says ("Mia's
  House!", "Keep out!", "Shop open"). Up to 60 letters, as big as they fit, on a wood, white,
  green or blue board. Right-click it later to change it. Everyone sees the same words, and rude
  words turn into ♥♥♥
- **Quick chat**: open the chat (T, or 💬 on a phone) and tap a phrase ("👋 Hi!", "😀 Follow
  me!", "🏠 Nice house!", "🏁 Wanna race?", "🆘 Help!"…) to say it without typing. Rude words in the chat turn into ♥♥♥ too
- **Dig into the ground**: three hits with the hammer dig out the grass. Holes are one block deep.
  Fill one back in with a Grass or Dirt block, or just wait: after a couple of minutes the grass
  grows back by itself (never while someone's standing in the hole). Or put **anything else** in
  it: water for a little pond, glass for a window in the ground, a lamp, a flower pot. Then it
  stays (break what's in it and it's a plain hole again). Digging on someone else's land isn't
  allowed
- **Secret tunnels** ⛏️🤫: under the ground is solid earth, 8 blocks deep, then the bedrock (which
  nothing can break). Only **Tunnel Diggers** (the ⛏️ club: Digger Dot at Big Rock Quarry) can
  dig into it: from the bottom of a hole, hammer down (3 hits a block), then dig along, sideways
  and up, to make tunnels and secret rooms, and build anything down there. It's earth up top and
  rock further down. Nobody else knows: the server only tells Tunnel Diggers about the tunnels
  and everything in them, so to anyone else the bottom of a hole is solid as ever. The hole on top
  grows back over like any hole, hiding the way in; dig back up from below to get out. Fill a
  tunnel spot back in with Grass or Dirt
- **Teleporters carry things too**: the ball (it pops out of the next one and rolls off, and a pet
  fetching it dives in after it), your pet (it walks in, or comes along when you go through),
  water balloons in flight, and vehicles: drive into a teleporter (or land the helicopter on it)
  and you come out of the next one still going the same way
- **Boats on a lake**: a lake with a wooden dock (railings, lanterns at the end) and three
  motorboats tied up beside it. Boats float on any water, even water you place yourself, so you
  can build your own pond or canal. They drift a little in turns, lift their bow at speed, leave a
  white wake, rock gently when they're parked, and the shore stops them (you can't drive out of
  the water). Getting out puts you on the dock or the shore, not in the water
- **Skyscrapers and parking lots**: two glass towers (48 and 36 blocks tall) with a lobby, a floor
  every 4 blocks, an elevator up to the roof and railings around the top (the tall one has a helipad
  on the roof: try landing the helicopter up there!), and two parking lots with lined spaces,
  lamps, a P sign and parked cars
- **Make your own character**: 10 faces, 8 hairstyles, 9 hats, 7 shirts, 4 kinds of pants,
  capes, wings and backpacks, and a color for every piece
- **Pets** that follow you, wander around and sniff things when you stand still, play fetch with
  the Pet Ball, and jump for joy when you pet them (you can pet other people's pets too). The ball
  flies in a big arc with a little trail and a shadow under it, and bounces off the ground, walls,
  railings, cars and people (with a boing and a puff of dust) before rolling to a stop. Pets chase
  it like real puppies: they watch where it goes first, run past it and swing back round, and a
  bouncing ball sometimes gets away from them. Pets move for real: they never walk through
  walls, closed doors, railings or cars, but find their way around them. They hop up steps (a
  little crouch, a leap, and a bump as they land), jump down drops of up to 3 blocks, and climb
  ladders paw over paw, so a ball thrown off a roof means a trip down the ladder and back up.
  If there's no way to reach you, your pet waits as close as it can get, looking up and whining;
  if the ball lands somewhere it can't reach, it gives up and the ball comes back to your hand.
  Dragons fly, so they go up and over walls instead. **Pets play together**: when your pet meets
  someone else's (and you're both nearby), they trot up for a nose-to-nose hello (with hearts),
  then play: tag (taking turns being "it", chasing and pouncing), zoomies round and round each
  other, and play bows (front down, tail wagging like mad) with bouncy pounces. Both pets' games
  keep time with the world's clock, so they take turns together on everyone's screen. After a
  good game they rest a little before the next one; throw the ball, or walk off, and they come
  running. Your pet comes along when you ride in a
  vehicle (or in the RV while somebody drives it) and hops out right beside you when you get
  out. If it ever gets stuck (in a block, underground, or shut in somewhere with no way out) or
  ends up more than 30 blocks away, it pops back in beside you with a little sparkle, on the
  ground where it can get to you
- **Character Gallery**: save your character (with your name) so anyone can pick it later.
  It holds 25 characters in a 5×5 grid; when it's full you choose one to replace, or replace the oldest
- **Rain, now and then**: not often (about one shower an hour, for a few minutes). The sky clouds
  over, the fog closes in and rain streaks down with little splashes (and you can hear it,
  muffled when you're indoors). It never rains inside: it stops at the roof (or a vehicle's roof),
  even over a hole in a roof like a skyscraper's shaft or a roof hatch. Skye reports it on BZ
  News, and when it stops there's a rainbow. (Up north it snows instead, and the snow stays out
  of the lodge and cabins now too)
- **Day and night**: the sun sets, the stars and moon come out and the street lamps light up.
  Sunrises and sunsets are soft and colorful (gold, orange, pink and red, with a round sun you
  can look at) instead of a blinding white glare.
  **Field of view** in the pause menu makes how wide you see narrower or wider (some people feel
  sick with the wrong one). Too dark (or not dark enough)? **Brightness at night** in the pause menu (Esc) makes the nights
  lighter or darker; the days stay the same
- **Police lights**: G in the police car turns on its lights and siren. The lights flash like the
  real thing (three quick red flashes, then three blue, with a glow round the light bar) and throw
  red and blue light over the street and the buildings around (on Medium and High graphics). Get
  out and the lights keep flashing (only the siren stops); off when you got out stays off
- **Cars parked around town**: regular cars, a police car with a siren and monster trucks that climb
  blocks, and two **sports cars** on the road down to the campground (low, light and quick). Out in
  the far country: **camper vans** (four seats, G pops the roof up, and you can sleep in them),
  **pickup trucks** (two in the cab and one riding in the back), **dump trucks** (G tips the dump
  bed) and the **RV** at the campground, a whole home on wheels: two seats up front, a dinette
  table, a couch, a kitchen, a TV, a bathroom and a big bed at the back, with windows to look in
  and out of; G rolls its awning out. **Walk right into the RV**: right-click it (or press E) and
  you step in through its door. Walk about inside (even while somebody else drives it down the
  road), and right-click things: sit on the couch, at the dinette or up front, flip through the TV
  channels over the windshield, cook something in the kitchen, get a snack from the fridge, wash
  your hands, roll the dice at the table, peek in the bathroom, or lie down in the big bed (B works
  too). Right-click the driver's seat to drive it (E gets you up and back into the RV), and walk
  out of the door (or press E) to leave. **Sleep in a camper van or the RV**: park the camper and
  press B (or tap 🛏️), or get into the RV's big bed, and move to get up. It counts like a bed for
  sleeping through the night, and if the van is yours (see below) it's your home: Go Home (R)
  takes you back to it. Cars drive right past
  little things like the lamps and lanterns along the roads, flower pots and traffic cones,
  instead of crashing into them, and hitting something solid only bounces you back a little, so
  you're soon on your way again. **Every kind of vehicle sounds like itself**: ordinary cars hum,
  the sports cars and the police car rasp and scream, the monster trucks and pickups burble like
  big V8s, the dump trucks and the RV clatter and whine like diesels, and the boats buzz like
  outboard motors. (They're recordings, see `tools/make-engine-sounds.py`; you hear other people's
  vehicles nearby too.) A 3-speed automatic you can hear: 1st revs up slowly from a stop, 2nd pulls to top speed,
  and 3rd is an overdrive that cruises at medium revs. If a car's corner gets caught on something it
  gently slides itself free. Brake lights come on when you brake (with a third light at the top of
  the back window), and when you hold S to back up the car stops, waits a moment, then switches on
  its white reverse lights and backs up. Two friends can ride along as passengers, and everyone
  climbs out beside the car, not on the roof. Crash hard and they dent, smoke, catch fire and blow
  up, then a new one shows up near its parking spot 30 seconds later
- **Fix it with the Wrench** (Tools tab): point it at a smoking car, boat or helicopter and click,
  or hold the button, to keep turning it. Each turn fixes a bit, knocks some of the dents back out
  and brings the shine back; it can even put out a fire if you're quick. The hint under the
  crosshair shows how healthy it is
- **Gas** ⛽: every car, truck and helicopter has a tank of gas that runs down as you drive (a car
  goes about 4,000 blocks on a full tank; big trucks and the RV use more). Boats don't need any.
  The gas gauge is next to your speed, and you get a warning at a quarter and at a tenth of a tank,
  with how far it is to the nearest gas station. Out of gas, the engine stops (a helicopter floats
  gently down). There are two **gas stations** by the roads out of town: park beside a pump, get
  out and right-click the pump to fill up. A whole tank costs 8 coins (less if it isn't empty; if
  you don't have enough coins you get as much as you can pay for). Ran dry somewhere far away?
  Walk to a gas station, right-click a pump with no car beside it to buy a **gas can** (3 coins, a
  quarter of a tank), carry it back (it's in your hand) and right-click your vehicle to pour it
  in. You can carry one can at a time. Town vehicles left nearly empty get filled up by the town
  after 10 minutes; your own vehicles are up to you
- **Mud and the car wash** 🧽: vehicles get dirty as you drive, slowly on roads, three times as
  fast on grass, sand and snow, and twice as fast again off the road in the rain. They get
  splashed with mud and lose their shine. Every gas station has a free **car wash**: drive through
  it slowly (about two seconds in the suds) and you come out sparkling
- **Paint any vehicle**: click a car, truck, boat or helicopter with the Paint Brush and it turns
  your paint color. It stays that color for everyone, and even comes back that color if it blows up
- **Real bumps (Newton's laws)**: when things collide they trade momentum. Ram a parked car and it
  gets shoved (and spins if you hit it off-center) while you slow down; heavy monster trucks barely
  notice small cars; knocked cars can knock into other cars; cars bump people out of the way; you
  can push a parked car by walking into it; and walking or driving into the Pet Ball kicks it
- **A helicopter** on the helipad east of town. It turns to face wherever you look (steer with the
  mouse), slides sideways with A and D, and banks into its turns like a real one. Fly it low and
  its wind blows dust (or sand, or spray off the water) out in a ring, tears leaves off trees,
  flattens the flowers and pushes the ball and people away. Easy to learn, tricky to master. If
  it's destroyed, a new one arrives after 5 minutes
- **Claim your own land** (up to 18 × 18 blocks) with the Land Flag so only you can build there
- **Logs grow into trees**: put a lone log on the grass and watch it grow
- **Build Book**: special things you can make out of blocks: beds, trampolines, campfires,
  teleporters, cannons, fountains, dance floors and TVs (with cartoons, news, weather, silly
  commercials, BZ Live, and since v0.49 the Space Show 🚀, Animal Friends 🦁 and a video game:
  **Block Hopper** 🎮. On the game channel the TV plays it by itself; right-click to play it
  yourself: Space, ↑ or tap to hop, twice for a double jump, over gaps and blocks, grabbing
  stars. Your best score is kept), and ten you can ride or play with:

can't turn or tilt until you've lifted off. Land gently, or
it gets damaged. Don't let the rotor hit anything!

## Running it on your computer

1. Install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (version 18 or newer).
2. Open a terminal in this folder and run:

   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

   ```bash
   npm start
   ```

3. Open <http://localhost:5010> in your browser.

To play with someone else on the same Wi-Fi, they can open `http://YOUR-COMPUTER-IP:5010`.

If the game feels slow, press Esc and set **Graphics** to Medium or Low. High has live
reflections, glow and soft shadows; Medium has cheaper versions; Low turns them off.

### Updating the game

Get the newest version with `git pull`, then stop the server (Ctrl+C in its terminal) and start
it again with `npm start`. Your world is kept (and backed up first if it needs upgrading).
Browser tabs that were already open notice the update when you press PLAY and load the new
version by themselves. If a game page ever acts strangely right after an update, reload it while
holding Shift (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

### Keeping your computer cool

Drawing a 3D world is hard work for a computer, so the game tries not to do more than it needs:

- **Frame rate** (in the Esc menu): 60 frames a second is smooth and the default. Choose
  **30 (cooler, saves battery)** if your laptop gets hot or loud; the game still plays fine.
  "As fast as possible" draws as many frames as your screen can show (only worth it on a
  gaming computer with a fast screen).
- While the game is paused, or a menu like the Build Book or the character editor is open,
  it draws only a few frames a second, so leaving it paused keeps things cool.
- Shadows and reflections are redrawn every few frames instead of every frame, there are
  fewer real lamp lights at once, small lamp models don't cast shadows, and smoke and sparkles
  only send the particles that are alive to the graphics card.
- On laptops with two graphics chips, the browser picks the cooler, battery-friendly one.
- Still hot? **Graphics: Medium** is much lighter than High, and **Low** is lightest of all.
- Blocks buried inside other blocks (like the inside of a mountain) aren't drawn at all, so the
  mountains cost about as much to draw as their surface.

### Running the server on a Raspberry Pi

A Raspberry Pi 4 runs the server fine. The world (about 115,000 blocks) takes about 40 MB of
memory, and the save file is about 1.4 MB. The server saves at most once every 15 seconds (not
after every block, which would wear out the SD card), and writes the file in the background, so
nobody's game stutters while it saves.

## Putting it online so friends can join

The game is a normal Node.js web app, so any host that runs Node works
(for example [Render](https://render.com), [Railway](https://railway.app) or [Fly.io](https://fly.io)):

- **Build command:** `npm install`
- **Start command:** `npm start`
- The server reads the `PORT` environment variable automatically.

Note: on free hosting the `data/` folder may be wiped on restart, which resets the world.

## Saved files

The server keeps everything in the `data/` folder:

- `data/world.json` is the world. To start over with a fresh town, stop the server, move or
  delete that file and start it again.
- `data/characters.json` is the Character Gallery. Delete it to empty the gallery.

**Updates never erase your world.** Everything players have built stays exactly where it is.
When a new version of the game needs to upgrade an older save, the server first copies it to
`data/world-backup-v<version>-<date>-<time>.json`, then only adds things: a helipad for the
helicopter (only where there's empty ground), lamp heads on the town's street lamps, road lines
turned to run along their roads, the skyscrapers and parking lots, and the lake with its boats
(built only on empty land that nobody has claimed: the landmarks beside the roads out of town or
along the ring road, the lake wherever there's room, smaller if it has to be), the Luxe Store
and the Pizza Palace (on empty land), the ski resort, the woods, the big lake and the mountains
(out past the old edge of the world, where nobody could build before; the roads out to them only
go on empty land, and where one meets an old road, just that bit of sidewalk becomes road),
the far country (out past the old edge of the 448 × 448 world, where nobody could build before:
the beach, the campground, the quarry, the farm and the rest of the ski mountain, with its roads
out there only on empty land, going round anything built or claimed, and only turning a bit of
sidewalk into road where one joins an old road), the weather station and the Build Park (on
empty land nobody has claimed), the "Build it here!" signposts (on empty land), fishing stools on the docks with a noticeboard
by each (only in empty spots, or among a dock's own railings, on land nobody has claimed),
and furniture: only in houses and towers that are exactly as the town was first
built (nobody has changed or claimed them), and only in their empty spots, and in the ski lodge
and the log cabins (their couches, beds, tables and the lodge's ski rack were missing before):
only onto their own plain wooden floor, and not in a cabin someone has claimed. The skyscrapers'
ladders become elevators, but only a tower's own ladder, exactly as it was built (from the ground
to the roof, up its concrete pillar) and only if nobody has claimed it; the Secret Agency's base
goes on empty land nobody has claimed, and its three TOP SECRET files only into empty spots (on a
floor, if there's one there) in the town's own places. If `world.json` can't be read, or was saved by a newer version of the game,
the server stops with a message instead of touching it.

## The Character Gallery

Open it from the main menu or the pause menu. Click a character to play as them: you get their
name and look. To save your own, type a name and press **Save my character**. Saving again
with the same name updates that character, so it works like a simple account. When all 25 spots
are taken, the game asks you to click one to replace, or to replace the oldest one.

If you press **Play** with a look that isn't saved yet, the game asks "💾 Save (your name)?", and
one click saves it: over the character with your name if there is one, otherwise in a free spot,
and if the gallery is full, over the character you're playing as (the one you last picked or
saved, even if you've renamed it; the question shows "🔁" and its name). Only when the gallery is
full and none of those fit does the gallery open, to pick which character to replace. Press **Not
now** to skip it. It won't ask again once that look is saved.

In single player (no server) the gallery is kept in your browser instead.
