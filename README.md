# BuildZone

A first-person, multiplayer creative sandbox that runs in the browser. Explore a town,
customize your character, drive cars, build anything and play with friends.

- **Towns to explore**: a main town around the spawn square plus four villages, joined by roads,
  with houses you can walk into, shops, parks, playgrounds, a beach and street lamps
- **Boats on a lake**: a lake with a wooden dock (railings, lanterns at the end) and three
  motorboats tied up beside it. Boats float on any water, even water you place yourself, so you
  can build your own pond or canal. They drift a little in turns, lift their bow at speed, leave a
  white wake, rock gently when they're parked, and the shore stops them (you can't drive out of
  the water). Getting out puts you on the dock or the shore, not in the water
- **Skyscrapers and parking lots**: two glass towers (48 and 36 blocks tall) with a lobby, a floor
  every 4 blocks, a ladder up to the roof and railings around the top (the tall one has a helipad
  on the roof: try landing the helicopter up there!), and two parking lots with lined spaces,
  lamps, a P sign and parked cars
- **Make your own character**: 10 faces, 8 hairstyles, 9 hats, 7 shirts, 4 kinds of pants,
  capes, wings and backpacks, and a color for every piece
- **Pets** that follow you, wander around and sniff things when you stand still, play fetch with
  the Pet Ball, and jump for joy when you pet them (you can pet other people's pets too). The ball
  flies in a big arc with a little trail and a shadow under it, and bounces off the ground, walls,
  railings, cars and people (with a boing and a puff of dust) before rolling to a stop. Pets chase
  it like real puppies: they watch where it goes first, run past it and swing back round, and a
  bouncing ball sometimes gets away from them
- **Character Gallery**: save your character (with your name) so anyone can pick it later.
  It holds 25 characters in a 5×5 grid; when it's full you choose one to replace, or replace the oldest
- **Day and night**: the sun sets, the stars and moon come out and the street lamps light up
- **Cars parked around town**: regular cars, a police car with a siren and monster trucks that climb
  blocks. A 3-speed automatic you can hear: 1st revs up slowly from a stop, 2nd pulls to top speed,
  and 3rd is an overdrive that cruises at medium revs. If a car's corner gets caught on something it
  gently slides itself free. Brake lights come on when you brake (with a third light at the top of
  the back window), and when you hold S to back up the car stops, waits a moment, then switches on
  its white reverse lights and backs up. Two friends can ride along as passengers, and everyone
  climbs out beside the car, not on the roof. Crash hard and they dent, smoke, catch fire and blow
  up, then a new one shows up near its parking spot 30 seconds later
- **Real bumps (Newton's laws)**: when things collide they trade momentum. Ram a parked car and it
  gets shoved (and spins if you hit it off-center) while you slow down; heavy monster trucks barely
  notice small cars; knocked cars can knock into other cars; cars bump people out of the way; you
  can push a parked car by walking into it; and walking or driving into the Pet Ball kicks it
- **A helicopter** on the helipad east of town. It turns to face wherever you look (steer with the
  mouse), slides sideways with A and D, and banks into its turns like a real one. Fly it low and
  its wind blows dust (or sand, or spray off the water) out in a ring, tears leaves off trees,
  flattens the flowers and pushes the ball and people away. Easy to learn, tricky to master. If
  it's destroyed, a new one arrives after 5 minutes
- **Claim your own land** (up to 10 × 15 blocks) with the Land Flag so only you can build there
- **Logs grow into trees**: put a lone log on the grass and watch it grow
- **Build Book**: special things you can make out of blocks: beds, trampolines, campfires,
  teleporters, cannons, fountains, dance floors and TVs (with cartoons, news, weather and
  silly commercials)
- **Doors** that open and close with a right-click (even with the hammer in your hand). Put two
  doors side by side and they become double doors that swing outward; put three or more in a row
  and they become one big garage door that rolls up. A garage door starts 3 blocks tall (so cars
  drive in under it when it's up); put another door on top and the whole garage door grows one
  block taller (4 tall fits a monster truck). Doors won't close on a parked car
- **Ladders**: point at a wall and click once, and the ladder runs from the ground all the way to
  the top of the wall. Walk into it and hold W or Space to climb (S to go down). Breaking any rung
  takes the whole ladder down
- **Railings and fences**: click near the edge of a floor (a stair step, a balcony, the ground or a
  sidewalk) to put a railing along that edge, or click the side of a wall to run one along its top.
  Click the other edges of the same spot for corners. A railing stops you walking (or falling, or
  jumping) off that side, but you can still step on from the other sides. It stops cars too, and you
  can paint it any color
- **Torches and lamps on anything**: lamps, lanterns and tiki torches stand on top of a sidewalk,
  road or carpet (instead of replacing it), and in the middle of a railing spot, so a fence can
  have lanterns on it. Doors can stand on a carpet too (put the carpet down first, or click a door
  with a carpet to slide one under it). The hammer (or paint brush) works on whichever part you
  point at: the crosshair hits the real shape of thin things like carpets, doors, ladders and
  lamps, so you can point past them at the wall behind
- **Light switches**: right-click a lamp to turn it off, and again to turn it back on. Street lamps
  look after themselves: on at night, off in the day
- **Road lines** run the way you're facing when you place them; right-click one to turn it. Parking
  lines work the same way
- **Paint anything, one side at a time**: the Paint Brush paints just the side of the block under
  the crosshair, so one block can have six different colors. Pick one of 15 paint colors, or the
  "+" swatch for any color you like. It works on carpets, doors, lamps and neon too (painted lamps
  shine in their new color). The Paint Scraper takes the paint off one side
- **Dancing** and **waving**
- **Fireworks** in 8 styles (including hearts and smiley faces) that light up the whole night sky
  and everything around them
- **Fancy lighting** (Graphics: High or Medium): shiny things like cars, glass, water, metal, gold
  and mirrors reflect the world around them, lamps and fireworks glow softly (no harsh glare),
  and shadows are soft
- **15 kinds of lights**, each with its own shape and color of light:
  - Indoor (on day and night): table lamp, floor lamp, ceiling light, chandelier, candle, lava lamp
  - Outdoor (switch on at dusk): lantern, street lamp, garden light, tiki torch, party lights
  - Glowing neon blocks in pink, blue and green, plus the classic Lamp block

  Lamps cast a soft glow that spreads wide around them, and the towns use them all: street lamps,
  porch lanterns, ceiling lights and table lamps in houses, garden lights and tiki torches in parks.
  Any lamp with a roof over it counts as indoors, so it lights up the room day and night. Windows
  are clear enough to see in and out, with just a little reflection
- **57 kinds of blocks** with realistic textures: brick, wood, glass, tinted glass, marble, roads,
  parking lines, flowers, rainbow, gold, mirrors, lamps, ladders, railings…
- **Sounds that don't get old**: every sound is a little different each time, every vehicle has its
  own engine and horn note, and a steady engine or helicopter hum slowly settles into the
  background (it comes right back when you speed up, slow down or climb)
- **Real-time multiplayer**: see each other, drive together, chat
- **Single player works too**: with no server, the world runs and saves right in the browser
- **Plays on phones and tablets** (held sideways): a joystick under your left thumb, drag to look,
  tap to build

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse | Look around |
| W A S D | Move |
| Space | Jump |
| Left click | Use the item in your hand (place a block, paint, throw the ball, launch fireworks) |
| Left click with the Hammer | Break a block: it takes 3 hits (glass and carpet break in 1), so you don't break things by accident |
| Right click | Interact: open and close doors, switch lights on and off, turn road lines, get in vehicles, pet pets, sleep in beds, fire cannons |
| Middle click | Copy the block you're looking at |
| 1–9 or mouse wheel | Choose hotbar slot. Slot 1 always holds the Hammer; anything you pick while slot 1 is selected goes in slot 2 |
| E | Open the item menu and the Build Book, or get in/out of a vehicle you're looking at |
| Shift | Sprint |
| F | Toggle flying (Space = up, C = down) |
| V | Switch camera: first person, behind you, in front of you |
| G | Dance! (on a dance floor it starts a party) |
| B | Wave |
| T or Enter | Chat |
| R | Go home: your bed, or your land if you have no bed, or else the town square |
| Esc | Pause menu: settings, character, Go Home, and Exit to Menu |

**Driving:** W to drive, S to brake (keep holding it once you've stopped to back up), A/D to
steer, Space to brake, Shift for a boost, H (or click) for the horn, G for the police siren, V to
change the camera, E to get out.

**Boats:** W to go, S to slow down and back up (boats have no brakes), A/D to steer (it turns
better the faster you go), Shift for a boost, H for the horn, E to get out.

**Riding along:** look at a car or the helicopter someone else is driving and right-click (or
press E) to hop in as a passenger. Up to two passengers fit. Passengers can look around and
change the camera with V, but only the driver can drive. Press E to get out.

**Flying the helicopter:** wait for the rotor to spin up, then hold Space to climb and Shift to go
down. Steer with the mouse: the helicopter turns to face wherever you look, and leans into the
turn (the faster you fly, the wider and swoopier the turn). W/S tilt forward and back (tilting
moves you, and you keep sliding until you tilt the other way). A/D slide sideways. Land gently, or
it gets damaged. Don't let the rotor hit anything!

**Railings:** pick the Railing, then point near the edge of a floor and click: a white outline
shows where it will go. Point at the side of a wall to put one along the top of the wall.

**Claiming land:** pick the Land Flag (slot 9), click one corner, click the opposite corner, then
click once more to claim it. Right-click cancels. With the flag in your hand, right-click twice to
give your land up. Land belongs to a name: if nobody plays with that name for 30 days, the land
becomes free again. Anyone can still walk in and use the doors.

## Playing on a phone or tablet

Open the game in the phone's browser (see below for the address) and turn the phone sideways.
When you press PLAY it goes full screen.

- **Walk:** put your left thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen and a joystick appears
  under it. Push a little to walk slowly, all the way to run.
- **Look around:** drag anywhere on the right side.
- **Use your item:** tap the right side (place a block, hit with the hammer, paint, throw the
  ball). Keep your finger still on the screen to keep hammering.
- **⬆** jumps (tap it twice quickly to start or stop flying; then **⬆** and **⬇** go up and down).
- **✋** opens doors, gets in cars, pets pets and sleeps in beds. In a vehicle it turns into **🚪**
  to get out.
- **Driving:** the joystick drives and steers (push it all the way up for a boost), **🛑** brakes.
  In the helicopter the joystick tilts and slides, **⬆**/**⬇** climb and descend, and it turns to
  face where you look.
- Tap a hotbar slot to pick an item. The round buttons at the top are pause **⏸** (with Go Home
  and Exit to Menu), items **🎒**, camera **🎥**, dance **💃** and chat **💬**.

Phones start with the Low graphics setting so the game runs smoothly; you can change it in the
pause menu.

## Running it on your computer

1. Install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (version 18 or newer).
2. Open a terminal in this folder and run:

   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

   ```bash
   npm start
   ```

3. Open <http://localhost:5010> in your browser.

To play with someone else on the same Wi-Fi, they can open `http://YOUR-COMPUTER-IP:5010`.

If the game feels slow, press Esc and set **Graphics** to Medium or Low. High has live
reflections, glow and soft shadows; Medium has cheaper versions; Low turns them off.

### Keeping your computer cool

Drawing a 3D world is hard work for a computer, so the game tries not to do more than it needs:

- **Frame rate** (in the Esc menu): 60 frames a second is smooth and the default. Choose
  **30 (cooler, saves battery)** if your laptop gets hot or loud; the game still plays fine.
  "As fast as possible" draws as many frames as your screen can show (only worth it on a
  gaming computer with a fast screen).
- While the game is paused, or a menu like the Build Book or the character editor is open,
  it draws only a few frames a second, so leaving it paused keeps things cool.
- Shadows and reflections are redrawn every few frames instead of every frame, there are
  fewer real lamp lights at once, small lamp models don't cast shadows, and smoke and sparkles
  only send the particles that are alive to the graphics card.
- On laptops with two graphics chips, the browser picks the cooler, battery-friendly one.
- Still hot? **Graphics: Medium** is much lighter than High, and **Low** is lightest of all.

## Putting it online so friends can join

The game is a normal Node.js web app, so any host that runs Node works
(for example [Render](https://render.com), [Railway](https://railway.app) or [Fly.io](https://fly.io)):

- **Build command:** `npm install`
- **Start command:** `npm start`
- The server reads the `PORT` environment variable automatically.

Note: on free hosting the `data/` folder may be wiped on restart, which resets the world.

## Saved files

The server keeps everything in the `data/` folder:

- `data/world.json` is the world. To start over with a fresh town, stop the server, move or
  delete that file and start it again.
- `data/characters.json` is the Character Gallery. Delete it to empty the gallery.

**Updates never erase your world.** Everything players have built stays exactly where it is.
When a new version of the game needs to upgrade an older save, the server first copies it to
`data/world-backup-v<version>-<date>-<time>.json`, then only adds things: a helipad for the
helicopter (only where there's empty ground), lamp heads on the town's street lamps, road lines
turned to run along their roads, the skyscrapers and parking lots, and the lake with its boats
(built only on empty land that nobody has claimed: the landmarks beside the roads out of town or
along the ring road, the lake wherever there's room, smaller if it has to be). If `world.json` can't be read, or was saved by a newer version of the game,
the server stops with a message instead of touching it.

## The Character Gallery

Open it from the main menu or the pause menu. Click a character to play as them: you get their
name and look. To save your own, type a name and press **Save my character**. Saving again
with the same name updates that character, so it works like a simple account. When all 25 spots
are taken, the game asks you to click one to replace, or to replace the oldest one.

In single player (no server) the gallery is kept in your browser instead.

## How the code is organized

```
server.js              Web server + multiplayer (WebSockets), saves the world
public/
  index.html           Page layout: menus, HUD, character editor
  style.css            Look of the menus and HUD
  js/
    main.js            Starts everything and runs the game loop: input, building, driving, camera
    hud.js             Hotbar, item menu, chat and info panels
    touch.js           Phone and tablet controls: joystick, look and tap, round pads
    textures.js        Generates all block textures in code
    world.js           Stores blocks, draws them in chunks, finds what you're looking at
    player.js          Walking, jumping, flying, collisions
    character.js       Builds a character from face/hair/hat/clothes choices
    editor.js          The "customize my character" screen
    gallery.js         The Character Gallery screen and its little portraits
    avatars.js         Other players' characters and name tags
    cars.js            Car and helicopter models, driving and flying physics, damage
    structures.js      Spots Build Book patterns (beds, teleporters…) in the world
    pets.js            What your pet does (follow, wander, fetch) and the ball
    landview.js        Draws land claim borders
    lighting.js        Live reflections and the glow effect
    lightpools.js      Soft pools of light on the ground under every lamp
    models.js          3D models for the lamps
    tv.js              The TV shows
    daynight.js        Sky, sun, moon, stars and night lighting
    items.js           Hotbar items, icons, the item in your hand
    effects.js         Particles, fireworks and sound effects
    network.js         Talks to the server (or runs single player)
    shared/            Code used by both the server and the browser
      blocks.js        The list of blocks
      avatar.js        Character options
      characters.js    The saved character list (25 spots)
      game.js          The game rules: blocks, players, vehicles, land, growing trees, the clock
      vehicles.js      Vehicle sizes, and finding a free spot to respawn them
      claims.js        Land claim rules
      worldgen.js      Builds the towns
```
